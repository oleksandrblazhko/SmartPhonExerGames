# 🎮 Game Genres and Examples of Gameplay Rules

## 1. Action Game (Skill-and-Action)

### 1.1 Fighting (Combat Games)
Example: Mortal Kombat

**Game Rules:**
- two players control characters in a confined arena;
- each character has a set of attacks, defenses, and special moves;
- victory is achieved by reducing the opponent’s health bar to zero;
- a round continues until one participant wins the required number of rounds.

### 1.2 Maze
Example: Pac-Man

**Game Rules:**
- the player moves through a maze on a fixed grid;
- all objects (dots) on the level must be collected;
- enemies pursue the player according to predefined algorithms;
- contact with an enemy results in losing a life;
- the level ends after all objects are collected.

### 1.3 Platformer
Example: Super Mario Bros.

**Game Rules:**
- the player controls a character in a two-dimensional environment;
- the character can move, jump between platforms, and avoid obstacles;
- contact with enemies or traps results in losing a life;
- the level ends when the final point is reached;
- some objects can be destroyed or collected.

### 1.4 Shooter
Example: DOOM

**Game Rules:**
- the player moves through the level environment and uses weapons;
- enemies appear in designated zones or in waves;
- hitting an enemy reduces its health resource;
- the player may lose health when taking damage;
- the level ends after completing an objective (eliminating enemies or reaching an exit).

### 1.5 Paddle Games
Example: Pong

**Game Rules:**
- the player controls a moving paddle;
- the objective is to return an object (ball) without allowing it to leave the field;
- each missed ball reduces the score or costs a life;
- the object moves according to a physical reflection model;
- victory is achieved by reaching a predefined score.

## 2. Role-playing Game
Example: Dungeons and Dragons

**Game Rules:**
- players control characters with attributes (strength, intelligence, etc.);
- interaction is governed by rules that determine action success (dice rolling);
- outcomes depend on character statistics and randomness;
- gameplay is structured by a scenario (campaign);
- character development occurs through experience accumulation.

## 3. Puzzle Game
Example: Tetris

**Game Rules:**
- pieces of different shapes fall into the game field;
- the player can rotate and move the pieces;
- completed horizontal lines disappear and award points;
- the falling speed gradually increases;
- the game ends when the field is filled to the upper boundary.

## 4. Adventure Game
Example: Monkey Island

**Game Rules:**
- the player explores the game world and interacts with objects;
- progress is achieved through solving logical puzzles and dialogues;
- important items can be collected and used in the correct context;
- the story advances through a sequence of scenes and events;
- fast reaction is not the primary success mechanism.

## 5. Racing
Example: Space Race

**Game Rules:**
- the player controls a vehicle in a competitive environment;
- the goal is to reach the finish first or complete the track in the best time;
- obstacles must be avoided and movement trajectory optimized;
- collisions with objects may slow the player down or push them off track;
- victory is determined by finishing order or completion time.
